# Building a Procedural Koi Pond in WebGL

> Placeholder content. This post exists to exercise the MDX pipeline and the
> shared typography components.

Every koi on this site is a **spring-driven spine** wrapped in a Catmull-Rom
body. No sprites, no rigs — just math resolved every frame.

## Some observations

A few things that made the pond feel alive:

- The water is captured into a multisampled buffer, then refracted in a single
  composite pass.
- Shadows use a premultiplied RG8 mask with painter-ordered casters.
- The pond drifts at a fraction of the page scroll speed for a slow parallax.
